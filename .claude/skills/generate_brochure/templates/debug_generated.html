Supercharge Innovation with AI-Powered Agile Design Thinking

About This Course

This course equips learners with the skills to integrate Agile design thinking methodologies with Generative AI (GenAI) to foster innovation and drive organizational growth. Participants will learn to use Design Thinking to frame problems, gather deep insights from end-users, and ideate creative solutions. The course emphasizes how Agile frameworks and Generative AI tools can accelerate the process of product development, iterating rapidly, and scaling innovative solutions across teams.

Learners will also develop proficiency in using project management tools and agile techniques to lead innovation initiatives, aligning stakeholder input to ensure successful implementation. They will gain an understanding of how Generative AI enhances traditional design thinking processes and will explore the latest trends in AI-driven innovation. By the end of the course, participants will be equipped to lead design thinking projects and strategically drive innovation across organizations.

What You'll Learn

By end of course, learners should be able to:

  • LO1 This learning outcome focuses on developing learners' ability to seamlessly integrate design thinking methodologies with agile principles while leveraging generative AI technologies to drive organizational innovation. Learners will understand the foundational concepts of design thinking, explore current trends in AI-powered innovation, and learn how to apply these integrated approaches in product development contexts to foster continuous innovation within their organizations.
  • LO2 This learning outcome enables learners to develop comprehensive innovation strategies by effectively gathering and synthesizing stakeholder insights to enhance products and services for organizational growth. Learners will master the art of stakeholder engagement, learn to identify key motivational factors, and translate these insights into actionable strategies that drive meaningful organizational development and competitive advantage.
  • LO3 This learning outcome develops learners' leadership capabilities in managing and executing design thinking projects across organizational boundaries. Learners will gain expertise in integrating design thinking methodologies into existing organizational processes, leading cross-functional teams in innovation initiatives, and applying project management tools to ensure successful delivery of innovation projects that drive organizational transformation and growth.
  • LO4 This learning outcome focuses on developing learners' analytical and strategic capabilities to synthesize complex information from multiple sources and stakeholders to gain deep understanding of end-user needs. Learners will master various project management tools and techniques while learning to transform user insights into comprehensive innovation strategies that align with organizational objectives and market demands.

Frequently Asked Questions

* What are the prerequisites for WSQ Funding?

  1. You need to be a Singaporean Citizen or Permanent Resident, physically based in Singapore.
  2. You must successfully complete the programme and pass the assessment in order to be eligible.
  3. You must attend at least 75% of the training.

* Can I club any other grant with this subsidy?

No, you cannot claim any other grant if you are claiming this subsidy from SSG. You should not be claiming for any other grants, subsidies, or tax concessions, provided unless explicitly permitted.

* Do I need to pay the full fee, and then claim the subsidy from WSQ Funding?

The programme works on a Nett fee model, i.e. you only need to pay the difference between the fee, and the funding amount at the time of enrollment. The training provider (TP) will claim the funding amount from SSG on completion of the programme. In case you fail to complete the programme, or if the claim raised by TP is rejected by SSG then you are liable to pay the funding amount to TP.

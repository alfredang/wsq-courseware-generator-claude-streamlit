Building Team Success Through Conflict Management Skills

About This Course

This comprehensive course provides essential skills for mastering conflict resolution, allowing learners to foster better collaboration and success within teams. Participants will learn to identify early indicators of conflicts and assess situations to minimize their impact. The course covers strategies for resolving conflicts effectively, applying communication and negotiation skills while remaining sensitive to team dynamics. Learners will also gain the ability to analyze and resolve workplace conflicts through coaching, ensuring that conflicts are addressed constructively.

Conflict resolution is critical in today's fast-paced workplace where diverse teams often experience challenges in collaboration. This course is highly relevant to industries such as management, human resources, and team leadership, where maintaining harmonious relationships and team effectiveness is vital. By completing this course, learners will be equipped to handle conflicts efficiently and prevent future issues, positioning them for roles that require leadership, conflict management, and team facilitation skills.

What You'll Learn

By end of course, learners should be able to:

  • LO1 Upon completion of this learning unit, learners will demonstrate the ability to proactively identify and assess conflict situations in the workplace. They will be equipped to recognize early warning signs, understand underlying causes, and validate information about conflicts to minimize their impact on team dynamics and productivity. This foundational skill enables timely intervention before conflicts escalate.
  • LO2 Upon completion of this learning unit, learners will possess the skills to analyze conflict situations, select appropriate resolution strategies, and implement them sensitively. They will demonstrate proficiency in communication and negotiation techniques, enabling them to resolve conflicts effectively while maintaining positive relationships and team cohesion.
  • LO3 Upon completion of this learning unit, learners will be capable of managing complex team conflicts within workplace environments. They will demonstrate the ability to discuss issues constructively, coach team members through conflict resolution processes, and apply organizational policies effectively to achieve positive outcomes for all parties involved.
  • LO4 Upon completion of this learning unit, learners will be skilled in evaluating the effectiveness of conflict resolution efforts and implementing strategies to prevent future conflicts. They will understand legal and ethical considerations in conflict management, enabling them to maintain professional standards while ensuring sustainable resolution outcomes.

No, you cannot claim any other grant if you are claiming this subsidy from SSG. You should not be claiming for any other grants, subsidies, or tax concessions, provided unless explicitly permitted.

* Do I need to pay the full fee, and then claim the subsidy from WSQ Funding?

The programme works on a Nett fee model, i.e. you only need to pay the difference between the fee, and the funding amount at the time of enrollment. The training provider (TP) will claim the funding amount from SSG on completion of the programme. In case you fail to complete the programme, or if the claim raised by TP is rejected by SSG then you are liable to pay the funding amount to TP.
